Where India’s Boardroom Leaders Meet Collegiate Ambition.

The Jai Hind Business Conclave (JBC) is the annual flagship business conclave organized by the Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) Department of Jai Hind College (Autonomous). Established to transcend conventional academic symposiums, JBC has emerged as a premier national forum connecting visionary executives, industry titans, and tomorrow’s business leaders.

Defined by an enduring standard of student governance and intellectual rigor, JBC has welcomed over 30 marquee CXOs, startup founders, venture capitalists, and economic policymakers alongside 8,000+ passionate student attendees. From candid keynote addresses with industry stalwarts to critical debates on financial engineering, artificial intelligence, and private equity, JBC constructs an intellectual arena where ideas transform into enterprise.

Everything you need to know about Jai Hind College (Autonomous), the BBA Department, and Jai Hind Business Conclave 2026-27.

Established in 1948 in South Mumbai (Churchgate), Jai Hind College (Autonomous) is one of India's foremost premier educational institutions. Renowned for academic excellence, alumni across top corporate leadership, and dynamic student-led initiatives, Jai Hind College consistently ranks among the top autonomous colleges affiliated with the University of Mumbai.

Jai Hind College is located on 'A' Road, Churchgate, Mumbai 400020, Maharashtra, India. Situated in South Mumbai near Marine Drive, the campus is within walking distance of Churchgate Railway Station and easily accessible via Western and Central railways, coastal roads, and Mumbai metro corridors.

The Jai Hind Business Conclave (JBC 2026-27) is the annual flagship business conclave organized by the Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) Department of Jai Hind College, Mumbai. JBC brings together industry leaders, founders, CXOs, venture capitalists, policymakers, and future corporate leaders for high-impact debates, fireside chats, and executive networking.

The BBA Department at Jai Hind College offers an industry-immersive curriculum designed to groom future executives, managers, and founders. Through flagship initiatives like JBC, students manage multi-million-rupee event operations, executive corporate relations, and high-level strategy under direct mentorship from college leadership and faculty mentors.

The conclave takes place in South Mumbai, hosting marquee keynote sessions and debates across the state-of-the-art Jai Hind College Auditorium (Churchgate, Mumbai 400020) and the luxury convention ballrooms at Vivanta by Taj President, Cuffe Parade, Mumbai.

Jai Hind College has hosted marquee business personalities including Mr. Keki Mistry (Interim Chairman, HDFC Bank), Mr. Dharmarajan (Founder & MD, Impactsure Technologies), Maulik Bhansali (Chief Risk Officer, Union AMC), Arjit Garg (Youngest SEBI Reg. Research Analyst), Rajat Bhatia (Commercial Director, Four Seasons), Annkur Khosla, Sahil Makhija, alongside faculty leadership Dr. Rakhi Sharma and Dr. Prateek Kumar.

Registrations for Jai Hind Business Conclave 2026-27 are open to college students, aspiring entrepreneurs, and industry professionals directly through the official registration portal on this site. Delegate passes grant access to speaker sessions, panel discussions, interactive Q&As, and curated networking lounges.

Jai Hind College is an autonomous institution accredited with an 'A' Grade by NAAC and affiliated with the University of Mumbai. It consistently ranks among Mumbai's top colleges for commerce, management studies, arts, and science education.
