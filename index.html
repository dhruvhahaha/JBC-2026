Everything you need to know about Jai Hind College (Autonomous), the BBA Department, and Jai Hind Business Conclave 2026.

Established in 1948 in South Mumbai (Churchgate), Jai Hind College (Autonomous) is one of India's foremost premier educational institutions. Renowned for academic excellence, alumni across top corporate leadership, and dynamic student-led initiatives, Jai Hind College consistently ranks among the top autonomous colleges affiliated with the University of Mumbai.

The Jai Hind Business Conclave (JBC 2026) is the annual flagship corporate summit organized by the Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) Department of Jai Hind College, Mumbai. Regarded as India's premier student-led business summit, JBC brings together industry leaders, founders, CXOs, venture capitalists, policymakers, and future corporate leaders for high-impact debates, fireside chats, and executive networking.

The conclave takes place in South Mumbai, hosting marquee keynote sessions and debates across the state-of-the-art Jai Hind College Auditorium (Churchgate, Mumbai 400020) and the luxury convention ballrooms at Vivanta by Taj President, Cuffe Parade, Mumbai.

JBC features marquee business personalities including Mr. Keki Mistry (Former Vice Chairman & CEO, HDFC Ltd), Dharamranjan (Chief Business Officer, Jio Financial Services), Maulik Bhansali (CEO, Netweb Software), Arijit Garg (Managing Director, Nomura), Rajat Bhatia (Founder, Katana Capital), Ankur Khosla, Sahil Makhija, alongside faculty leadership Dr. Rakhi Sharma and Dr. Prateek Kumar.

Registrations for Jai Hind Business Conclave 2026 are open to college students, aspiring entrepreneurs, and industry professionals directly through the official registration portal on this site. Delegate passes grant access to speaker sessions, panel discussions, interactive Q&As, and curated networking lounges.

The BBA Department at Jai Hind College offers an industry-immersive curriculum designed to groom future executives, managers, and founders. Through flagship initiatives like JBC, students manage multi-million-rupee event operations, executive corporate relations, and high-level strategy under direct mentorship from college leadership and faculty mentors.
